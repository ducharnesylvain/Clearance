# Supplier payouts policy

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy sets when and how Wandrel pays suppliers for completed bookings.

## 2. Timing

**2.1** Suppliers are paid for a booking after its activity date.

**2.2** The standard payout frequency is weekly, every Tuesday.

## 3. Changing frequency

**3.1** Suppliers may request a monthly payout frequency.

**3.2** A frequency change takes effect from the next payout cycle.

## 4. Frequency options

**4.1** Available frequencies are weekly, every two weeks, and monthly.

**4.2** Suppliers in their first 90 days on the platform are paid weekly.

## 5. Amounts and currency

**5.1** Balances below 50 EUR, or the equivalent in another currency, are not paid out and roll over to the next cycle.

**5.2** Payouts are made in the supplier's local currency where our payment provider supports it.

**5.3** Our payment provider currently supports payouts in EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, JPY and MXN.

## 6. Early payouts

**6.1** Early payouts, before the activity date, may be offered to suppliers with more than 12 months on the platform and an average rating of 4.5 or higher.

**6.2** Early payouts are capped at 30% of the booking's supplier net amount and require approval by the Finance Operations Lead.
