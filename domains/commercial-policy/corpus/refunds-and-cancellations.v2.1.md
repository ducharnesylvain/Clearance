# Refunds and cancellations

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers cancellations by travellers and suppliers, and the refunds that follow.

## 2. Traveller cancellations

**2.1** By default, travellers may cancel for free until 24 hours before the activity.

**2.2** Suppliers may mark a product as non-refundable. This must be shown to the traveller before checkout.

## 3. Supplier cancellations

**3.1** If a supplier cancels a booking, the traveller receives a full refund, including the service fee.

**3.2** Wandrel pays refunds to travellers and recovers the supplier's share from the supplier's next payout.

## 4. Goodwill

**4.1** Customer service may grant a goodwill refund of up to 100 EUR per booking. Larger amounts require approval by the Customer Service Lead.
