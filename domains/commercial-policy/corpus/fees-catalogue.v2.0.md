# Traveller fees catalogue

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This catalogue lists every fee Wandrel may charge travellers, on top of the retail price.

## 2. Service fee

**2.1** Wandrel may charge travellers a service fee of 0% to 5% of the retail price.

**2.2** The service fee must be shown to the traveller before checkout.

**2.3** Wandrel does not charge surcharges for the payment method used.

## 3. New fees and refunds

**3.1** Any new traveller-facing fee requires approval by Legal and Finance before launch.

**3.2** Service fees are not refundable.

## 4. Tax

**4.1** Traveller fees are subject to indirect tax as set out in the taxes and invoicing policy.
