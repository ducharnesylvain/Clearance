# Revenue recognition

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy sets when and how Wandrel records revenue from bookings. It follows simplified, fictional rules made for this project.

## 2. Pricing models

**2.1** Under the commission model, Wandrel recognises its commission as revenue on the activity date.

**2.2** Under the net-rate model, Wandrel recognises the difference between the traveller price and the supplier net rate as revenue on the activity date.

## 3. Markups and fees

**3.1** Markups are recognised as platform revenue and reported separately from supplier cost.

**3.2** Traveller service fees are recognised as revenue on the activity date.

## 4. Discounts

**4.1** Platform-funded discounts are booked as marketing expense and do not reduce recognised revenue.

## 5. New money flows

**5.1** Any commercial change that creates a new money flow must be mapped to accounting events approved by the Financial Controller before launch.
