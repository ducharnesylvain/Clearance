# Vouchers and gift cards

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers gift cards sold to travellers and compensation vouchers issued by Wandrel.

## 2. Gift cards

**2.1** Gift cards sold to travellers are a liability until they are redeemed or expire.

**2.2** Gift cards are valid for 24 months from purchase.

## 3. Compensation vouchers

**3.1** Compensation vouchers issued by Wandrel are platform-funded and booked as marketing expense when redeemed.

**3.2** Compensation vouchers are valid for 12 months. A voucher worth more than 50 EUR per booking requires approval by the Customer Service Lead.

## 4. Channels

**4.1** Gift cards may not be sold through the reseller or affiliate channels.
