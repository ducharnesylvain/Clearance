# Commission policy

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy sets the commission Wandrel keeps on bookings sold under the commission model.

## 2. Standard rates

**2.1** The standard commission is 22% of the retail price of each booking.

**2.2** Attraction tickets carry a commission of 15%.

## 3. Individual rates and changes

**3.1** An individual commission rate between 12% and 30% may be agreed with a supplier and approved by the Supply Lead.

**3.2** Commission changes may apply retroactively to bookings made in the previous 30 days, if the supplier agrees in writing.

## 4. Calculation

**4.1** Commission is calculated on the retail price, excluding the traveller service fee.
