# Pricing policy

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers how retail prices are set on Wandrel, for both pricing models and all channels.

## 2. Pricing models

**2.1** Under the commission model, the supplier sets the retail price and Wandrel keeps a commission.

**2.2** Under the net-rate model, the supplier sets a net rate and Wandrel sets the retail price by adding a markup.

**2.3** The net-rate model is available to every supplier with a signed contract.

## 3. Price integrity

**3.1** A supplier's retail price on Wandrel may not be higher than the price the supplier shows on its own website for the same product and date.

**3.2** Wandrel may not set a retail price below the supplier net rate, except through a platform-funded promotion allowed by the discounts and promotions policy.

## 4. Net-rate markups

**4.1** Markups on net-rate products must be between 10% and 30% of the supplier net rate.

**4.2** The markup is fixed per product and per season, and recorded in the pricing system before sale.

## 5. Reseller channel

**5.1** Reseller channel markups may not exceed 8% of the supplier net price.

**5.2** Channel markups must be disclosed to resellers in their rate sheet.

## 6. Price changes

**6.1** Retail price changes of more than 20% within 24 hours require approval by the Pricing Lead.
