# Approval matrix

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This matrix lists who approves commercial changes before launch. Each policy remains the source for the rule itself.

## 2. Approvers

**2.1** Individual commission rates between 15% and 35%: Supply Lead.

**2.2** Commission rates outside 15% to 35%: Commercial Committee.

**2.3** Reseller commission above 12% and affiliate commission above 8%: Commercial Committee.

**2.4** Retail price changes of more than 20% within 24 hours: Pricing Lead.

**2.5** Platform-funded promotions below the supplier net rate: Marketing Lead, through a campaign budget.

**2.6** Early payouts and reseller credit limit increases: Finance Operations Lead.

**2.7** New traveller-facing fees: Legal and Finance.

**2.8** New money flows and new accounting event types: Financial Controller.

**2.9** New traveller currencies: Treasury.

**2.10** Changes to the seller of record: Tax Lead.

## 3. Changes no policy covers

**3.1** A change that no policy covers must not be launched. Raise it with the Policy Owner group.
