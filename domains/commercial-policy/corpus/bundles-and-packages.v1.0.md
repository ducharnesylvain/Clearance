# Bundles and packages

## 1. Scope

**1.1** A bundle combines two or more products from at least two different suppliers, sold to the traveller as one booking.

## 2. Revenue allocation

**2.1** The bundle price is allocated to each product in proportion to its standalone retail price.

**2.2** Each supplier is paid according to its own pricing model on the allocated amount.

## 3. Discounts and eligibility

**3.1** A bundle discount may not exceed 15% of the sum of the standalone retail prices.

**3.2** The bundle discount counts towards the total discount limit of the discounts and promotions policy.

**3.3** Products from suppliers in their onboarding period may not be included in bundles.
