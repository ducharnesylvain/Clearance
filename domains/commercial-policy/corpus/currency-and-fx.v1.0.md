# Currency and FX

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers the currencies used by travellers and suppliers, and who carries exchange-rate risk.

## 2. Currencies

**2.1** Travellers may pay in EUR, USD, GBP, CHF or JPY.

**2.2** Supplier contracts are denominated in EUR, USD or GBP.

**2.3** Supplier payouts are made in the supplier's contract currency.

## 3. Exchange-rate risk

**3.1** On direct channel bookings, Wandrel carries the exchange-rate difference between the traveller currency and the supplier contract currency, recorded as FX_GAIN_LOSS.

## 4. Changes

**4.1** Adding a new traveller currency requires approval by Treasury.
