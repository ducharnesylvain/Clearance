# Supplier onboarding

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers new suppliers from contract signature to the end of their onboarding period.

## 2. Checks before payout

**2.1** Identity and business checks must be completed before a supplier's first payout.

**2.2** A supplier's first payout is released no earlier than 14 days after its first activity date.

## 3. Onboarding period

**3.1** The onboarding period is the first 90 days after contract signature.

**3.2** Suppliers in their onboarding period may only use the commission model.

## 4. Commercial terms during onboarding

**4.1** Individual commission rates may only be agreed after the onboarding period ends.
