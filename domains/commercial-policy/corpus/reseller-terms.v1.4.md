# Reseller channel terms

## 1. Scope

**1.1** Resellers are hotels, travel agencies and other businesses that resell Wandrel products to their own customers.

## 2. Payment terms

**2.1** Suppliers selling through the reseller channel may be paid monthly.

**2.2** Resellers settle their account with Wandrel monthly in arrears.

## 3. Reseller commission

**3.1** Resellers buy Wandrel products at the retail price minus a reseller commission.

**3.2** The reseller commission is between 8% and 12% of the retail price. A higher commission requires approval by the Commercial Committee.

## 4. Rate parity

**4.1** Resellers may not sell Wandrel products below the Wandrel retail price.

## 5. Credit

**5.1** Each reseller has a credit limit set by the Finance Operations Lead. Any increase requires the Finance Operations Lead's approval.
