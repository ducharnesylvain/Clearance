# Commission policy

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy sets the commission Wandrel keeps on bookings sold under the commission model, on the direct channel.

**1.2** Net-rate products are covered by the pricing policy, not by this policy.

## 2. Standard rates

**2.1** The standard commission is 25% of the retail price of each booking.

**2.2** Category rates replace the standard rate: attraction tickets 15%, multi-day tours 18%, all other tours and activities 25%.

**2.3** Suppliers in their onboarding period pay the category rate for their products.

## 3. Individual rates and changes

**3.1** An individual commission rate between 15% and 35% may be agreed with a supplier and must be approved by the Supply Lead. Rates outside this range require approval by the Commercial Committee.

**3.2** Commission changes apply only to bookings made after the change takes effect. Retroactive changes are not allowed.

**3.3** Every individual rate is recorded in the supplier contract before it is applied.

## 4. Calculation

**4.1** Commission is calculated on the retail price, excluding the traveller service fee.

**4.2** Commission rates may not vary by the traveller's nationality or country of residence.
