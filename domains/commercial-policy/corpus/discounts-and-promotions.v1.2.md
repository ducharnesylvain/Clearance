# Discounts and promotions

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers promo codes, campaign discounts and any reduction of the retail price shown to travellers.

## 2. Who pays

**2.1** Platform-funded discounts are paid by Wandrel and booked as marketing expense. The supplier receives its normal payout.

**2.2** Supplier-funded discounts reduce the supplier's payout and require the supplier's written consent before launch.

## 3. Limits

**3.1** Only one promo code may be applied per booking.

**3.2** The total discount on a booking may not exceed 40% of the retail price.

## 4. Below-net promotions

**4.1** A platform-funded discount may bring the traveller price below the supplier net rate only with a campaign budget approved by the Marketing Lead. The supplier still receives the full net rate.

## 5. Targeting

**5.1** Discounts may not be restricted by the traveller's nationality, passport or country of residence.

**5.2** Discounts may be restricted by product, date, channel or minimum basket value.
