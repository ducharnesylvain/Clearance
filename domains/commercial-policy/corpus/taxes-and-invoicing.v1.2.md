# Taxes and invoicing

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy uses simplified, fictional tax zones A, B and C. It does not describe any real tax law.

## 2. Invoices and tax

**2.1** Wandrel is the seller of record and issues traveller invoices in tax zones A and B. In tax zone C, the supplier issues the invoice.

**2.2** Traveller fees carry indirect tax at the rate of the traveller's tax zone.

## 3. Changes and business customers

**3.1** Any change to who is the seller of record requires approval by the Tax Lead.

**3.2** Resellers receive one business invoice per month.
