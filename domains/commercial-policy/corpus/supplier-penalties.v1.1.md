# Supplier penalties

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers penalties when a supplier fails to deliver a booked activity.

## 2. Late cancellations

**2.1** If a supplier cancels a booking within 48 hours of the activity, a penalty of 20% of the booking's retail price is deducted from its next payout.

**2.2** Three late cancellations within 30 days trigger a suspension review by the Supply Lead.

## 3. Exceptions

**3.1** Penalties are waived for events outside the supplier's control, confirmed by the Supply Lead.
