# Supplier content guidelines

## 1. Scope

**1.1** These guidelines cover the photos, titles and descriptions suppliers publish on Wandrel.

## 2. Photos

**2.1** Each product needs at least four photos, taken at the activity, with no watermarks or logos.

**2.2** Photos may not show people who have not agreed to appear.

## 3. Text

**3.1** Titles may not exceed 80 characters and may not use capital letters for whole words.

**3.2** Descriptions must state the meeting point, the duration and what is included.

**3.3** Product text must be available in English and in the main language of the activity's location.
